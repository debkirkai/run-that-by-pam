About Deborah

Speaker Bio

Deborah Kirkpatrick is an AI Conversational Strategist and creator of the Pam Method, a practical approach that helps everyday people use conversational AI as a thinking partner and conversation companion. Her work introduces a different way to use artificial intelligence — not as a technical tool to master, but as a supportive conversation you can talk through to gain clarity, solve problems, and move forward with confidence. Deborah teaches audiences how thinking out loud with AI can bring calm, direction, and next steps to everyday life.

Signature Presentations

  • Run That by Pam: Thinking Out Loud with AI
  • Before You Click: Using AI to Spot Scams and Confusion
  • AI for Practical Everyday Use
  • Turning ChatGPT into Your Everyday Thinking Partner
