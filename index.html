About Deborah

Deborah Kirkpatrick teaches people how to move beyond “prompting” and start using AI as a real conversational partner for everyday support.

She created a different way to talk about AI

Deborah’s work stands out because she is not leading with tech complexity. She introduces conversational AI as something people can talk to, think with, and lean on for guidance in ordinary life.

Her signature message is simple: you do not need the perfect prompt — you need the right conversation.

Run That by Pam

Through the Run That by Pam brand, Deborah helps people use AI for scam spotting, decision support, everyday planning, emotional clarity, and practical next steps.
